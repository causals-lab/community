# Contributing to Causals Lab

Thank you for helping improve Causals. You can contribute without writing code.

## Choose a contribution

1. **Graph correction or addition:** open a graph proposal issue, or use the contribution form on causals.org when it becomes available. Include the exact node/edge link or stable ID, the proposed change, and supporting sources.
2. **Review:** examine a proposal's evidence, meaning, scope, and possible counterevidence. Explain your reasoning; a vote alone is less useful.
3. **Ontology:** propose a term or relationship-type change in a discussion before submitting a pull request to its specification.
4. **Benchmark:** propose a question, answer criteria, source set, and foreseeable shortcuts or leakage risks. Do not publish private test answers in an open issue.
5. **Research and documentation:** raise a question or submit a focused pull request.

## For a graph proposal

Please include:

- Stable IDs or links for the affected entities and relationships, if available.
- The precise claim or proposed edit, including direction, scope, and time period.
- At least one accessible source with title, URL or identifier, and the passage or data relevant to the claim.
- Whether the source supports an **association**, a **causal hypothesis**, an **identified causal effect**, or another relationship; explain any uncertainty.
- Counterevidence, confounders, or alternative explanations you know of.
- Whether the text or data can lawfully be shared and reused. Link to a source instead of copying substantial copyrighted material.

We may ask for clarification, mark a proposal as disputed, or decline it with a reason. Acceptance is not an endorsement of every statement in a cited source. Published graph records should retain provenance and review history.

## Ground rules

- Do not submit credentials, personal secrets, private documents, or unlawfully obtained data.
- Do not use issues or discussions to run bulk imports or automated LLM submissions. A separate, rate-limited ingestion workflow is planned.
- Disclose when a proposal or review was materially generated by an LLM, including the model if known. Check every cited source yourself.
- Be precise and respectful when disagreeing. Critique claims and evidence, not contributors.

The review workflow and contribution terms are being finalized before broad ingestion opens. See [OPENNESS.md](OPENNESS.md) for current publication boundaries.
